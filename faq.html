Frequently Asked Questions

What is Free Hosting?
Free Hosting is a platform that provides free hosting for Discord bots, powered by Pterodactyl infrastructure.
How do I host my Discord bot?
Create an account, then create a server in the dashboard. Upload your bot files and start the server.
What programming languages are supported?
We support Node.js and Python for Discord bot hosting.
What is Pterodactyl?
Pterodactyl is a server management panel that we use to power our hosting infrastructure.
How do I upload my bot?
You can upload files directly through the file manager, or connect a Git repository.
Can I restart my bot?
Yes, you can start, stop, restart, and kill your bot from the server management page.
What happens if my bot crashes?
You can configure automatic startup in the settings to restart your bot automatically after crashes.
What are the resource limits?
Resource limits are provided by your hosting configuration and vary by plan.
Is the hosting really free?
Yes, we offer free hosting for Discord bots with basic resource allocations.
